Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh (1853-1890) was a painter from the Netherlands. He painted with thick paint and bright colors, and today he is one of the most famous artists in the world.

Claude Monet

Claude Monet (1840-1926) was a French painter who helped start the Impressionist movement. He often painted outdoors to capture sunlight, rivers and flowers.
